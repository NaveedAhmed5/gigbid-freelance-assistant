**Key Technologies:** PostgreSQL, AWS, TypeScript, Redis

3. **Execution:** Implemented modular components allowing progressive rollout with zero downtime.

- **Quantifiable ROI:** Demonstrated a 32.0% measurable efficiency improvement within 60 days of production deployment.
