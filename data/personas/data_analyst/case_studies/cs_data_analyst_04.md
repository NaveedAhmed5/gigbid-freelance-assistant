# Case Study: Automated Compliance & Redaction Service
**Project ID:** cs_data_analyst_04 | **Industry:** Media | **Role:** Lead Engineer
**Key Technologies:** PowerBI, SQL, Python, Excel

### Problem Statement
The client experienced severe operational bottlenecks in their media workflows, resulting in degraded user satisfaction, escalating infrastructure overhead, and manual intervention.

### Architecture & Technical Solution
1. **Core Architecture:** Deployed a modern event-driven solution leveraging PowerBI, SQL, Python, Excel.
2. **Deterministic Guarantees:** Enforced strict schema validation, automated recovery fallbacks, and comprehensive telemetry.
3. **Execution:** Implemented modular components allowing progressive rollout with zero downtime.

### Measurable Business Outcomes
- **Performance Impact:** Designed PII detection and rule-based redaction compliant with GDPR/HIPAA.
- **Quantifiable ROI:** Demonstrated a 28.0% measurable efficiency improvement within 60 days of production deployment.
- **Client Endorsement:** Solution adopted as the core foundation for subsequent enterprise scaling.
