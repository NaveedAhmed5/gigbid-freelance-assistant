# Case Study: Zero-Downtime Database Migration Framework
**Project ID:** cs_data_analyst_15 | **Industry:** Gaming | **Role:** Lead Engineer
**Key Technologies:** A/B Testing, Snowflake, Python, Tableau

### Problem Statement
The client experienced severe operational bottlenecks in their gaming workflows, resulting in degraded user satisfaction, escalating infrastructure overhead, and manual intervention.

### Architecture & Technical Solution
1. **Core Architecture:** Deployed a modern event-driven solution leveraging A/B Testing, Snowflake, Python, Tableau.
2. **Deterministic Guarantees:** Enforced strict schema validation, automated recovery fallbacks, and comprehensive telemetry.
3. **Execution:** Implemented modular components allowing progressive rollout with zero downtime.

### Measurable Business Outcomes
- **Performance Impact:** Migrated 2TB relational database across regions without customer disruption.
- **Quantifiable ROI:** Demonstrated a 100.0% measurable efficiency improvement within 60 days of production deployment.
- **Client Endorsement:** Solution adopted as the core foundation for subsequent enterprise scaling.
