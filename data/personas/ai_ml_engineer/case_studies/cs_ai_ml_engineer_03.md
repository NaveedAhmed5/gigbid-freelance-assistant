# Case Study: Real-Time Fraud & Anomaly Detection Gateway
**Project ID:** cs_ai_ml_engineer_03 | **Industry:** LegalTech | **Role:** Lead Engineer
**Key Technologies:** vLLM, PyTorch, Transformers, Python

### Problem Statement
The client experienced severe operational bottlenecks in their legaltech workflows, resulting in degraded user satisfaction, escalating infrastructure overhead, and manual intervention.

### Architecture & Technical Solution
1. **Core Architecture:** Deployed a modern event-driven solution leveraging vLLM, PyTorch, Transformers, Python.
2. **Deterministic Guarantees:** Enforced strict schema validation, automated recovery fallbacks, and comprehensive telemetry.
3. **Execution:** Implemented modular components allowing progressive rollout with zero downtime.

### Measurable Business Outcomes
- **Performance Impact:** Implemented streaming risk assessment flagging anomalous behaviors within 20ms.
- **Quantifiable ROI:** Demonstrated a 50.0% measurable efficiency improvement within 60 days of production deployment.
- **Client Endorsement:** Solution adopted as the core foundation for subsequent enterprise scaling.
